A portrait film lives in the story. On the way to it, a sponsored frame arrives first.

The shows have ended, and the clothes that held up on the street are the ones that stayed with us. This season the silhouettes were quieter, the fabrics heavier, and the color almost always pulled from the weather.

Scroll to the video

We followed one look from the fitting room to the sidewalk, and shot it the way it actually moved: unhurried, and a little windblown.

The week itself was a run of early calls and late rooms. Designers talked about weight, about what a coat can carry when the day is long, and about color that looks like stone, milk, and wet pavement.

None of that fits in a still. The record of it is one look, followed from the rack to the street, with the sound of the city left in.

What follows is that film. It plays in the story, then makes room for the sponsor, then picks up where it left off.
